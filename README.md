# Muse Tune-Up — starter kit for a new Muse

A clean, shareable tune-up for someone setting up their own Muse.
It gives their Muse the same operating discipline a well-run Muse has —
without anyone else's personal data, projects, accounts, or secrets in it.

## What's in here

- `START-HERE.md` — the one prompt to paste to your Muse. It runs the
  whole tune-up on itself, in order, and reports back with proof.
- `TUNE-UP-CHECKLIST.md` — the 10 checks the tune-up runs, and what
  PASS / FIX / SKIP means for each.
- `templates/` — starter identity files your Muse adapts to *you*:
  - `SOUL.md` — how your Muse behaves and what it never does without asking
  - `IDENTITY.md` — its name, character, and vibe
  - `USER.md` — who you are (filled in by asking you, never guessed)
  - `MEMORY.md` — a lean long-term memory file, kept current-state only
  - `AGENTS.md` — working conventions: verify before claiming, batch
    independent work, keep memory lean, scan before publishing
- `SECURITY.md` — the non-negotiables: secrets handling, prompt-injection
  rule, verify-before-publish, and re-checking any action whose outcome
  is unknown before repeating it.

## How to use it (2 minutes)

1. Send this whole folder (or repo) to your Muse — attach the zip, or
   paste the files into a chat with it.
2. Paste the prompt from `START-HERE.md` as your message.
3. Your Muse will ask you a few setup questions (your name, timezone,
   what it's allowed to do without asking), run the checklist, apply the
   templates adapted to you, back up the originals first, and report
   each check as PASS / FIXED / SKIPPED with evidence.
4. Nothing gets published, spent, deleted, or connected to an account
   during the tune-up without your explicit OK for that specific thing.

## What it deliberately does NOT do

- No other person's data, projects, wallets, or accounts — this kit is
  generic on purpose. Your Muse fills it in from *your* answers only.
- No auto-installing random tools. Anything the checklist flags as
  "worth adding" is a suggestion with a verdict, never a silent install.
- No spending, trading, posting, or account changes. Those stay gated
  behind your explicit approval, every time — that's in the templates.

## Keeping it tuned

Re-run the checklist monthly, or after any big change (new connectors,
new skills, a lot of new memory). A tune-up is a habit, not a one-off.
