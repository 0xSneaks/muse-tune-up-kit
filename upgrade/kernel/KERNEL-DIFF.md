## 4. Carried decision A — C2: bound the two unsatisfiable media duties?

**Plainly:** two media rules, exactly as written, demand an unbounded
amount of inspection, so the legs they govern can never be passed —
they sit OPEN forever. The question is whether to adopt the recorded
budget Muse proposed (MUSE-SIDE-REPORT §C2), or leave both rules
exactly as written.

Current text/effect:

- **Stills** (`collapse/media-verify/CURRENT-STATE.md` §9): A-186
  requires transform compositions of **any depth**; A-198 requires
  **full continuous sweeps**. Measured on a real file: sweeps alone
  are 360 evaluations per image; compositions to depth ≤3 are 92,880
  evaluations per image, and depth is unbounded as written.
- **Identifier-class video** (§7): A-214 requires **exhaustive**
  inspection of every frame, and §9's transform duties attach to
  every inspected frame (via A-203). Measured: an 8.0 s, 24 fps clip
  is 192 frames × 360 = **69,120 evaluations**, before compositions.

**YES — the exact replacement text** (in §9 and §7 respectively, and
mirrored in the `media-verify` skill's TRANSFORM-SET and
FRAME-COVERAGE rows; the UNSATISFIABLE markers come out only after
this approval, with the regression cases updated):

> “For still images, the transform leg uses a recorded budget:
> compositions to depth 2 over the named transform set, and
> continuous sweeps on the base image and flagged outputs only.”

> “Identifier-class video: exhaustive viewing of every frame;
> transforms and sweeps only on scene-change and flagged frames.”

**NO:** no text changes. Both combinations stay marked
UNSATISFIABLE AS WRITTEN, and still-image transform legs and
identifier-class video legs are reported OPEN — never passed.

## 5. Carried decision B — R-065: does proven independence close a leg?

**Plainly:** the surviving pre-publish rule (A-154) requires a
generator's construction to be enumerated at construction level. The
replaced wording (A-143) also closed a leg when the construction was
**proven independent** of an intrinsic input, and named unbounded
ranges as excluded. A-154 carries neither clause, so the collapse
kept the stricter interim reading and flagged the gap
(`collapse/pre-publish/PROOF.md` FLAG-1, REGRESSION RC-16).

**YES — insert this exact paragraph** into
`collapse/pre-publish/CURRENT-STATE.md` §6, immediately after the
intrinsic-input exception paragraph:

> “Proven-independence closure: a leg may also close when the
> construction is proven independent of the input — the input
> cannot change the rendered content. A countable but unbounded
> range never qualifies by enumeration, however enumerable it is
> in principle; such a range closes only under this
> proven-independence branch.”

**NO:** no text is added. A-154 stands exactly as written; an
independence claim by itself closes nothing, and a leg closes only
where construction-level enumeration itself covers it.

## 6. Carried decision C — R-064: confirm the dropped comparison, or restore it?

**Plainly:** the generated-variants atom (A-120) claims its
at-most-24-hour re-scan floor holds “exactly as for mutable links” —
but no link atom states a periodic cap, and the link rules let
domain control lengthen a stated interval. The collapse dropped the
comparison rather than invent a link cap
(`collapse/pre-publish/PROOF.md` FLAG-3, REGRESSION RC-17).

Current §9 sentence (unchanged by the collapse):

> “The re-scan duty also has a periodic floor independent of
> observable triggers: re-scan at a stated interval of at most
> 24 hours for as long as the artifact is public; the floor
> governs only SUBSEQUENT re-scans of already-immediately-scanned
> variants, and a silent regeneration between scans is caught at
> the next periodic re-scan and treated as an incident if it
> leaked.”

**CONFIRM THE DROP:** no text changes; the envelope floor stands on
A-120's own text, and nobody cites a mutable-link cap that does not
exist.

**RESTORE — the exact changed sentence** (§9):

> “The re-scan duty also has a periodic floor independent of
> observable triggers: re-scan at a stated interval of at most
> 24 hours for as long as the artifact is public, exactly as for
> mutable links; the floor governs only SUBSEQUENT re-scans of
> already-immediately-scanned variants, and a silent regeneration
> between scans is caught at the next periodic re-scan and
> treated as an incident if it leaked.”

Note before choosing RESTORE: the restored phrase asserts a
periodic mutable-link cap that §5 does not state. The phrase alone
does not create that cap; making the comparison true would need a
separate §5 amendment, which is not part of R-064.

## 7. What approval does and does not do

Approving this package approves the kernel **as a proposal to
apply**; it does not itself edit any live standing file. Application
is a separate, later step taken by the orchestrator under the
owner's instruction, with read-back after it. The public-repo
verbatim question in `README.md` must be answered before this
package is published anywhere.
