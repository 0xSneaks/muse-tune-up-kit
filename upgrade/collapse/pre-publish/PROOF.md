## Flags (named, never silent)

- **FLAG-1 — GAP (R-065), decision owed.** The replaced
  intrinsic-input wording (A-143) allowed closure when the
  construction is proven independent of the input, and named
  unbounded ranges as excluded. The surviving atom (A-154)
  carries neither clause in terms. The pair map's resolution
  asks for a decision ("decide whether proven independence
  still closes a leg and whether unbounded ranges are excluded
  by name; write it into the survivor"). This collapse carries
  A-154 as written and applies the conservative interim reading
  (an independence claim closes nothing unless the
  construction-level enumeration itself covers it); it does not
  invent the missing clause. The owner/orchestrator decision is
  owed. The interim reading is the stricter one, so no leg
  closes that the surviving text does not close — but the
  replaced text's express closure route is unaccounted for
  until the decision lands.
- **FLAG-2 — GAP (A-091), no collapsed home yet.** A-091's
  observable-types clause (a cross-check corroborates only leg
  types the source can observe) has no home in any lane draft:
  its parent, verification-core §3, does not carry it, and the
  pair map's SINGLETONS row assigns it to that parent rather
  than to this skill. Recorded here so the atom is accounted
  for; the fix belongs in verification-core (the same pattern as
  deploy-verify's FLAG-6 for R-052).
- **FLAG-3 — DECISION introduced by this collapse (R-064).**
  A-120 claims its 24-hour envelope floor holds "exactly as for
  mutable links", but the link atoms state no periodic cap and
  let domain control lengthen the interval. The collapse drops
  the comparison instead of inventing a periodic link cap; the
  envelope floor stands on A-120's own text, and the only
  24-hour bound on link re-checks is the one A-066's own base
  text states (the first delayed re-check, and always before
  promotion). Nothing is loosened — no link atom ever contained
  the cap the comparison asserted — but the choice is this
  lane's and wants the reviewer lane's confirmation (the same
  pattern as deploy-verify's FLAG-4).
- **FLAG-4 — HOMING resolutions, content exactly once.** (a)
  A-220 is registry-homed in this domain but was collapsed by
  deploy-verify §4 with A-212, per R-054/R-080 and this lane's
  brief; §11 cross-references it rather than re-collapsing.
  (b) A-183 is in this domain's PUB-GENERATED-ENVELOPE chain but
  its operative rule is the thumbnail rule in media-verify §4;
  §9 states only the precedence pointer. (c) A-192 runs the
  other way: media-verify's PROOF names pre-publish as its
  primary text, so its general rule is collapsed in §9 and
  media-verify §4 carries only its media clauses.
- **FLAG-5 — RESOLUTION CHOICE (R-007).** The pair map offers
  two resolutions for the A-218/A-192 conflict: limit the
  provisional route to non-auto-distributing platforms, OR
  require owner acceptance of the capture risk. §9 states the
  limit as the rule and routes the acceptance through §10's
  full waiver chain (per R-055), so an acceptance, where the
  owner gives one, is a recorded waiver and never a gate pass.
  This is the stricter composition of the two offered options;
  flagged for the reviewer's confirmation.
- **FLAG-6 — SEND ATOMS NOT COLLAPSED HERE.** A-085, A-089,
